# Intake questions

Ask only what you cannot infer. Group into a single message, at most 6 questions. If the user says "just draft it" or is not around, state assumptions at the top of the brief and continue.

## Must know (build cannot proceed without these)

1. **What do you sell, to whom, and at what price?** One sentence. Needed to compute LTGP and to fill every script.
2. **What advertising are you doing today, and how much of it per day or week?** Channels, volume, and what it currently produces (leads, calls, customers). Volume is the first diagnosis; do not skip it.
3. **More time than money, or more money than time?** Plus roughly how many hours per day they can commit and any ad budget. This alone decides the primary method.

## Very useful

4. **The warm audience size**: phone contacts, email lists, followers per platform, past customers. Warm outreach and lookalike targeting both start here.
5. **Their numbers, if known**: LTGP or rough profit per customer, current CAC, churn, referral percentage. Say it is fine not to know; unknown numbers become the brief's first measurement tasks rather than invented figures.
6. **What they could give away**: existing assets (guides, audits, templates, trial capacity, spare service hours) that could become a lead magnet without new build cost.

## Nice to have

- Industry average CAC if they know it (for the 3x diagnostic).
- Real capacity limits (spots, hours) for honest scarcity in CTAs.
- Platform they already consume and understand best (paid ads platform checklist).
- Whether they want the brief as markdown only or also .docx or .pdf.

## Critique mode

Ask for the actual material (the ad, script, posts, funnel screenshots) if not pasted, plus the numbers behind the complaint: sends per day, reply rate, spend, cost per lead, close rate. Do not guess these. If they say "it doesn't work", ask how much of it they did and for how long; the answer usually is the finding.
