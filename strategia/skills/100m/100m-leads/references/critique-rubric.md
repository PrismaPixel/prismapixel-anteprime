# Critique rubric for existing advertising

Score every applicable line 0, 1, or 2. Quote the user's own material as evidence wherever possible. Lines 1 to 8 apply to every critique; lines 9 to 15 apply per method, so only score the block matching the material. Present the scorecard as a table, name the single biggest leak, then rebuild.

## Universal lines (score all)

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Volume vs the book's prescription | Sporadic or unknown; nowhere near 100/day or equivalent | Regular but well under prescribed volume | At or near Rule of 100 / Open To Goal levels, sustained |
| 2 | Aimed at engaged leads, not vanity | Optimizes impressions, likes, "brand awareness" | Collects contacts without qualifying interest | Every action drives a measurable show of interest with contact captured |
| 3 | Lead magnet or core offer clarity | No clear offer anywhere | An offer exists but is vague or weak | A specific core offer or lead magnet that solves a narrow problem and reveals the next one |
| 4 | Give:ask balance | All ask, no value given first | Some value but ratio near 1:1 | At least 3:1 give to ask; big fast value up front |
| 5 | CTA quality | None, or "learn more" | Clear action, no reason to act now | Clear action plus honest scarcity, urgency, or reason why |
| 6 | Inputs and outputs tracked | Nothing measured | Outputs eyeballed, inputs untracked | Daily inputs committed and funnel metrics known at each step |
| 7 | Economics | CAC and LTGP unknown | Known but LTGP:CAC below 3:1 with no plan | At or above 3:1, or a credible plan (raise LTGP, 30-day cash) to get there |
| 8 | Right problem diagnosed | Blames the channel with no evidence | Partially diagnosed | Correctly separates advertising vs sales vs product problems |

## Reading the total

Score the universal block out of 16 and the method block out of 6.
- Universal 0 to 6: the machine is missing; rebuild from Stage 1 of the build.
- Universal 7 to 12: a channel with leaks; fix the lowest lines in this order: volume, offer/lead magnet, CTA, tracking, economics.
- Universal 13 to 16: strong; the wins are in More-Better-New and lead getters.

## Common complaints and the likely leak

| Symptom | Likely leak | First fix |
|---|---|---|
| "This channel doesn't work" | Volume (line 1) | Compute their actual daily inputs vs Rule of 100; usually a fraction of it |
| "Lots of followers, no customers" | No lead magnet or ask (lines 3, 5) | Build the lead magnet, add intermittent asks at 3:1+ give:ask |
| "Leads but no sales" | Wrong diagnosis (line 8) | If leads are qualified with money, it is a sales problem, not more leads |
| "Ads too expensive" | Economics (line 7) | Compare CAC to 3x industry average; fix ads if above, fix LTGP if below |
| "Cold email gets no replies" | Line 10c | Bigger, faster value; the book 3x'd take rates by giving real free service instead of a disguised sales call |
| "Content grew then stalled" | Give:ask drifted (line 4) | Count recent gives vs asks; over-give again |
